# Isola del Giglio model: data sources

- Building footprints (with mapped heights), streets, paths, piers, coastline and woods: © OpenStreetMap contributors, Open Database Licence (ODbL), https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright. Extract of 6 November 2026.
- Terrain: AWS Terrain Tiles (Terrarium encoding; sources include SRTM, GMTED2010 and ETOPO1; Mapzen), https://registry.opendata.aws/terrain-tiles/.
- Colours, roofs, windows, trees and landmarks are styled by tools/model3d/build_village3d.py (style: mediterranean).
